# Daily crew and final-scene upgrade — October 6, 2026

Today's transcript had three governance/risk stories, a nominal location handoff
without a distinct scene experience, sparse reciprocal warmth, and spoken planning
language. Earlier prompts and structural checks did not establish finished quality.

## Changes

- Shared crew direction now reaches storyboard, writing/editing through the shared
  editorial brief, final scene review, and performance planning. Each story requests
  host intentions, emotional movement, a reciprocal relationship beat and a sourced
  discovery. Prior episode patterns remain available; invented intimacy is prohibited.
- Headline classification recognizes task forces, reporting requirements and local
  oversight. A second listener-frame axis groups governance and security together.
  This activates the existing bounded diversity refill when all three cluster;
  it retains the lead and prioritizes comparably trusted alternatives. It does not
  invent positive stories, replace major news with trivia, or guarantee tonal variety.
- One extra bounded text review runs after runtime expansion/trim and before the
  existing fact audit. It proposes at most 12 exact local repairs and records specific
  scene findings. Numeric changes, sponsor edits, removed feature navigation, partial
  lines, cross-scene changes and new structural/runtime failures are rejected locally.
  Provider failure retains the existing script and reports the failure.
- Internal planning-language leakage is now a final hard script check, including all
  hosts. Daily production has HARD_FAIL_PRE_TTS=true; unresolved leakage stops before
  new TTS spending. A failed candidate remains available for repair.
- The performance planner receives the date, daily scene briefs and recent patterns
  with the exact final script. Its existing validated per-turn moods reach the voice
  router. Approved voices and provider routing are unchanged. Sponsors remain neutral.
- crew_review_report.json is retained in workflow artifacts. Its findings describe
  the model's pre-repair assessment, not an independent post-repair listening verdict.

## Validation and limits

63 offline regression tests passed, including 8 new crew tests. Provider calls were
mocked. Python compilation passed. No paid episode or marketing asset was generated.

This release is an implementation, not proof of 9.5/10 entertainment. Final audio,
location-feature audibility, natural laughter, host chemistry, subscriber response
and the next scheduled run remain to be verified. Mood coverage/fallbacks remain
visible in performance_plan.json and hybrid_tts_report.json. The final scene review
adds one application-level text call; existing provider SDK retry behavior remains.

The fact audit retains its existing behavior; numerical preservation is not semantic
fact verification. Current story classification remains a heuristic, with explicit
concentrated-slate reporting when a credible alternative is unavailable.
