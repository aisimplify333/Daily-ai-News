About the show

Understand the news. Enjoy the company.

The AI Edge is Daniel Wabs’s weekday AI news and analysis podcast for curious people who want to understand what artificial intelligence changes in everyday life, work and business.

We explore the developments that matter, question the claims and bring the conversation back to something useful. Expect different perspectives, room for disagreement, and enthusiasm when there is something worth getting excited about.

Alex

The curious, clear-headed host. Asks the question you were thinking and brings the discussion back to you.

Jamie

The human stakes and the spark. Quick, warm and ready to challenge a claim—or celebrate a good idea.

Rufus

The money, the incentives and the raised eyebrow. A global perspective with dry English understatement.

Alex, Jamie and Rufus are AI-voiced characters. The show uses AI-assisted research and writing. Analysis and predictions are perspectives, not guarantees.
