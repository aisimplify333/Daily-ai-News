## Native Spotify distribution
RSS publication is not completion of Spotify-native promotion.
- Select one 30-60 second captioned video clip per episode that establishes a real
  topic, demonstrates responsive cast chemistry and delivers a small payoff.
  Avoid unsupported scientific/financial claims, staged testimonials and sponsor reads.
  Review the actual rendered clip before upload. A generated MP4 in the repository
  is not evidence that Spotify received it.
- Prepare one question tied to the episode and 2-7 distinct answer choices.
  Today's prepared poll is listener_poll.json. Publish only through an authorized
  Spotify Creators operation; record the episode, publication status and observed
  vote counts separately. Do not claim an unpublished poll is available.
- Use actual poll results and submitted questions for concise future callbacks.
  State sample size when discussing a small vote; do not manufacture consensus.
- Review the content of actual comments before drafting a reply or discussing them
  on air. The existence of a comment alone does not establish its sentiment.
- Maintain one clear follow invitation, without piling on competing calls to action.

## Measurement
Keep private Spotify screenshots and actual audience metrics out of this public repo.
Maintain date windows and metric definitions with every private observation.
Track impressions, discovery conversion, new/returning audience, episode retention,
follows gained, clip-to-episode behavior where available, and attributable newsletter
signups or qualified inquiries. Compare episodes at equal ages, e.g. seven days.
Do not mix discovery-attributed plays with total plays, average consumption time
with per-listener duration, or 90-day consumption with 30-day eligibility.
Small denominators make 0%/100% episode results unstable. Example metrics files are
fixtures, not actual business results. Do not feed screenshot aggregates into
episode-level automation or a production quality gate.

## Commercial sequencing
Keep the main show freely discoverable while testing utility and repeat listening.
Use a useful companion resource for Jamie's feature (source links, steps and limits)
to give listeners a reason to visit the newsletter. Track actual episode-specific
referrals and signups before claiming conversion or sponsor ROI.
Consider relevant disclosed affiliate offers or a small direct sponsorship pilot
only after checking audience fit and a clear deliverable; never sell unsupported reach.
Protect independent reviews and avoid adding ad load before retention is understood.
Spotify Partner Program is a later channel, not evidence of current revenue eligibility.
Do not migrate hosting solely because the dashboard advertises monetization.
Any migration requires an explicit plan preserving episode GUIDs, archives,
distribution and the automated publishing process.
