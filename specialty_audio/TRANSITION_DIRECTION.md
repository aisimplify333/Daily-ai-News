# Transition sound direction
Updated October 19, 2026 at Daniel's request.

## Editorial intent
Use variation when it improves pacing and listener interest. Keep one recognizable
sonic family, not unrelated effects at every break. Preserve the established opening
and closing and Rufus's distinct field-dispatch cue.

## Next sound package
Prepare and audition three related ElevenLabs transition variants:
- Main story change: the familiar signature motif, short and clear.
- Jamie's Try This: a brighter, playful variation, communicating discovery without
  a novelty horn, cartoon effect or forced laughter.
- Reflective or serious story change: a restrained, softer version of the motif.

Choose by the actual editorial context. Do not randomly rotate sounds or put an
upbeat sting immediately after distressing material. Silence or a short pause can
be the better transition. Avoid identical adjacent stings when a suitable approved
alternative exists. Keep transitions brief and speech intelligible; compare loudness
and listen to each variant in context with the cast before enabling it.

Jamie retains her own excited entrance; the sting must not cover her first words
or turn a useful review into an advertisement. Rufus's location ambience should
match an explicitly imagined setting and remain distinct from story transitions.

## Current implementation status
The daily package currently loads one transition asset and reuses it. This document
records the requested next-package direction; it does not enable new sound assets
or claim that audible variation has shipped. Generation, in-context review,
selection logic, per-transition asset reporting and rendered-audio verification
remain necessary before marking this complete.
