# AI changes the story. Know what it means for you.

Status: written production package; new performances and animated shots NOT generated. Existing v1 has nine portrait-motion video drafts and separate audio/captions. Listening approval remains pending. No social publication is authorized or claimed here.

## Performance contract

Sell the pleasure of understanding and trying AI. Every scene moves from a recognizable situation through disagreement or surprise to a useful possibility. Jamie finds the opportunity; Rufus challenges it with affection; Alex turns the exchange into a clear listener benefit. No invented customer results, testimonials, statistics or urgency. These are explicitly dramatized AI-cast exchanges.

Alex: Charon, warm American gravitas; curious rather than promotional. Jamie: Ursa, responsive, playful, willing to disagree; a small spontaneous laugh where earned. Rufus: Iapetus, brisk contemporary southern English, dry mid-register, clipped punchlines; never an imitation of Alex. Preserve the established voices. ElevenLabs stock voices must not silently replace them.

Conversational direction: vary tempo within an exchange. Leave a short beat before a punchline and move on immediately afterwards. One laugh at most per short ad; no laugh track. Keep music below every word. Start with a person and a question, not a logo animation. End with one CTA. Keep the faces and existing identity.

## Three flagship scenes

### The Monday Meeting

Hook: "What would you try if you had one hour back?"

- ALEX [curious, leaning into the question]: What would you try if you had one hour back?
- JAMIE [delighted, quick]: That idea you've been putting off. A rough design. A first draft. Something that actually exists outside your head.
- RUFUS [dry, slight upward eyebrow]: Dangerous. We might have to stop calling it a strategy and make something.
- JAMIE [small laugh, affectionate challenge]: Come on. Even you have an idea you're excited about.
- RUFUS [warm concession]: Several. I ration the enthusiasm.
- ALEX [warm, confident payoff]: That's The AI Edge. The possibilities, the questions, and something useful to take into your day.

Visual beats: Alex question close-up; Jamie leans forward; Rufus eyebrow reaction; quick return to Jamie; Rufus half-smile; Alex payoff. An abstract sketch-to-prototype illustration may appear briefly, clearly illustrative rather than a product performance claim.

### Too Many Headlines

Hook: "Twelve AI headlines. Which one is worth your time?"

- JAMIE [comic overload, then curiosity]: Twelve AI headlines. Which one is worth my time?
- RUFUS [deadpan]: The one that doesn't describe itself as a revolution. A small but promising field.
- ALEX [amused, then practical]: Let's ask a better question. What might this help you do?
- JAMIE [brightening]: Learn something difficult. Try an idea. Get past the blank page.
- RUFUS [warm, lightly teasing]: You're allowed to be excited. I shall inspect the small print.
- ALEX [inviting]: Bring both to The AI Edge. Curiosity and a few good questions.

Visual beats: quick stylized headline cards that do not invent real news; cut to Rufus on "revolution"; cards clear away as Jamie lists possibilities; settle on cast and a single CTA.

### Would You Trust AI With That?

Hook: "Would you let AI plan your holiday?"

- ALEX [playful, genuinely asking]: Would you let AI plan your holiday?
- JAMIE [enthusiastic]: Ideas? Yes. An itinerary? Let's see it. Booking the whole thing without me? Steady on.
- RUFUS [dry]: Particularly if your relaxing afternoon involves three airports.
- JAMIE [brief laugh, then practical]: Exactly. Be adventurous. Check the details.
- ALEX [warm payoff]: That's the conversation. Where AI opens a door, where you stay in charge, and why it matters to your life.

Visual beats: cast reaction cuts; three simple airport icons briefly collide; clear immediately for Jamie's practical line. Never present this as a real holiday or customer experience.
