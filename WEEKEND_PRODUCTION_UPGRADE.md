## Review the actual programme

Listen once without reading the script and mark exact moments of confusion, amusement,
surprise, fatigue and desire to share. Then check those timestamps against the transcript
and source records. Review the opening, longest discussion, transitions, sponsor and close
on headphones and a phone speaker. No automated 97/100 can substitute for this.

Record separate findings for factual grounding, story variety, cast distinction, responsive
chemistry, pacing, sound and payoff. A synthetic-audio test verifies signal handling only.
No retention improvement, listening score or chart position is established by this release.
