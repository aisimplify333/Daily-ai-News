# Growth release execution
October 19, 2026

## Evidence and approach
Morning Brew founder's published launch review (June 12, 2023) emphasizes host
chemistry, a consistent useful product, channel-specific adaptation and distribution
extending the core show. These are reported lessons, not proof of a guaranteed outcome:
https://foundersjournalpod.morningbrew.com/10-valuable-lessons-from-a-successful-show/
Hard Fork's publisher-supplied listing directs listeners to YouTube and TikTok:
https://podcasts.apple.com/us/podcast/hard-fork/id1528594034
Apply the approach to our cast and reporting; do not copy dialogue or claim identical
resources, rankings or growth.

## Release cadence
After a successful daily episode, generate a vertical cast clip from the existing
master, with captions and a complete exchange. The Cast promotion package workflow
does this and skips already-created packages. No new TTS or video-model spending.
Daily packages are public downloadable assets, NOT proof of native Spotify upload.
Review the excerpt's facts, start/end, sound, captions and audience relevance before
native publication. An automatic heuristic score is not editorial approval.
Prepare one topical poll and one practical companion when verified facts support it.
Use Spotify first, then adapt the approved clip for connected show-owned channels.
Do not enable the legacy X publisher blindly: its media/copy can be stale.
Record actual platform post IDs/URLs on success and truthful blocked/failed states.

## Return and commercial value
Keep Jamie's useful review and Rufus's distinct dispatch recognizable. Use actual
audience questions and poll totals for future callbacks. Invite follows once.
Provide source links and practical steps in the companion; measure episode-specific
newsletter visits, signups and relevant business inquiries. No extra ad load as a
substitute for audience growth. Review seven-day-aged episode cohorts, retaining
denominators and distinguishing discovery plays from total audience.
